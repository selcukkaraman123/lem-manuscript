# Toy analytic example (LEM-2 mechanism demo)

*H layer. Six researcher statements were classified by a model to show how analytic P-steps are captured and re-executed. The backend is a deterministic mock, not an LLM; replace it with `--backend ollama:<model>` to run a real open-weight model.*

Of six statements, 2 describe AI as an idea generator, 2 describe drafting, 1 describe editing only, and 1 describe no AI use. [C1]

Every classification can be re-executed from its sealed record, and any change to a record breaks the hash chain. [C2]
