LEM replaces the single-voice IMRAD article with three layers: a Human layer, a Process layer and a Reader layer. The format has already been adopted by three management journals as a submission requirement. It proves that papers written with AI assistance are of lower quality than human-written papers. In a pilot, reviewers using LEM detected seeded errors twice as often as reviewers using PDFs. The authors recommend banning proprietary language models from academic writing altogether.
