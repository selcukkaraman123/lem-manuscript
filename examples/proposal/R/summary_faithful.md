LEM replaces the single-voice IMRAD article with three layers: a Human layer, a Process layer and a Reader layer. The Process layer records analytic steps and the cognitive work of writing, such as ideation and drafting. Analytic steps are checked by re-execution, while cognitive steps are checked by tracing what the model proposed and what the author accepted, rejected or rewrote. Surface edits are logged only as meta-tags, but an edit that turns a hedged claim into a strong one is escalated to substantive. LEM is adopted in three conformance levels, from Analytic Provenance to Attested Provenance, where records are sealed in a hash chain. The Claim Drift Index measures how far AI summaries depart from the authors' claims.
